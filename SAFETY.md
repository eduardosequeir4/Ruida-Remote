# Safety instructions

Ruida Remote controls moving laser machinery. Incorrect use can cause injury, fire, equipment damage or unexpected laser emission.

- Operate only when physically present at the machine and able to reach its **physical emergency stop**.
- Never rely on the app's stop/release command as the sole safety measure. Wireless connections can fail or be delayed.
- Keep guards, interlocks, extraction, cooling and other manufacturer-required protections functional.
- Keep hands, tools and bystanders away from moving axes. Confirm the travel area is clear before jogging.
- The laser pulse command can emit hazardous radiation. Use only with the enclosure and protective measures specified by the machine manufacturer.
- Never bypass safety interlocks or leave the machine unattended.
- Verify the machine's configured travel limits; this app does not guarantee software-enforced boundary protection.
- If communication fails or motion/emission continues unexpectedly, use the **physical emergency stop** and follow the manufacturer's shutdown procedure.

This software is an independent remote interface, not a certified safety controller.
